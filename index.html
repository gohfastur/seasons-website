About

A focused, long-term property owner.

Seasons Property Holdings is a privately held real estate company focused on quality residential rentals in Southern California. We acquire homes we believe in and hold them for the long term.

Our model is simple: own good properties, manage them attentively, and build lasting relationships with the residents who make them home.

  • ✦Long-term ownership. We buy to hold, not to flip.
  • ✦Resident-first service. Responsive, respectful, and clear.
  • ✦Quality standard. Homes maintained to a high bar.
  • ✦Local focus. Rooted in Orange County and Southern California.
